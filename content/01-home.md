Frequently Asked Questions
Everything You Need To Know
What health concerns can Payal help with?
Payal's work focuses on gut health, weight management and personalised nutrition. This can include concerns such as bloating, acidity, digestive discomfort, stubborn weight and lifestyle-related nutrition challenges. The appropriate guidance depends on your individual health needs.
How is Payal's gut-first approach different from a regular diet plan?
Rather than focusing only on calories or food restrictions, Payal considers your gut health, eating habits, lifestyle, individual needs and relationship with food. The aim is to build a more personalised and sustainable approach to nutrition.
Can I work with Payal if my main goal is weight loss?
Yes. Weight management is one of Payal's core areas of expertise. Her approach considers your food habits, lifestyle and relevant health factors instead of relying solely on restrictive dieting.
What's included in the 4-Week Nutrition Experience?
The planned experience brings together guided nutrition, recipes, habit tracking, selected gut-focused wellness products and WhatsApp-based support. Final programme inclusions will be shared before enrolment.
Can I consult Payal personally?
Yes. Payal offers private consultations for individuals seeking more personalised nutrition guidance based on their health history, goals and lifestyle. You can enquire about availability through the website.
Are the nutrition plans customised to individual needs?
Personalisation is central to Payal's philosophy of bio-individuality. Private guidance takes your nutritional needs, preferences, routine and health goals into account. The four-week programme has a separate guided format.
Will I have to stop eating my favourite foods?
Not necessarily. Payal's approach focuses on developing healthier and more sustainable food habits rather than placing everyone on the same restrictive diet. Any specific changes depend on your needs.
Are vegetarian, vegan or other dietary preferences considered?
Dietary preferences can be discussed when enquiring about personalised nutrition guidance. Any allergy-related or medically necessary restrictions should also be shared before starting.
Do I need to take GutAvatar products to follow Payal's approach?
GutAvatar is a separate range of gut-focused wellness products. It is not a substitute for personalised nutrition or medical care. Whether a particular product is suitable depends on your needs and its ingredients.
Can I invite Payal for a corporate event or wellness workshop?
Yes. Payal is available for enquiries related to corporate wellness sessions, workshops, clubs and leadership forums, covering subjects such as gut health, nutrition, weight management and the gut-brain connection.
How do I get started?
Explore the four-week programme, visit GutAvatar, enquire about private consultations or contact the team about a speaking engagement. Choose the option that best matches what you're looking for.
